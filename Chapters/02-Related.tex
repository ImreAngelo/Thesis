\chapter{Preliminaries}
\label{chp:protocol}

\vfill
\gls{spar} is assembled from standard \gls{fhe} components, and this chapter presents each of them in the form the rest of the thesis uses. \autoref{sec:notation} fixes the common notation. \autoref{sec:fhe} introduces homomorphic encryption and the BGV scheme, together with the multi-party variant that provides the protocol's threshold decryption. \autoref{sec:rns} presents the residue number system that practical BGV implementations compute in. \autoref{sec:gadget-decomposition} defines the gadget decomposition, \gls{rgsw} ciphertexts and the external and internal products. \autoref{sec:spar} presents the \gls{spar} construction itself and the requirements it places on the scheme beneath it.
