\begin{itemize}
    \item By \eqref{def:rgsw}, $C = Z + \mu G$ with $G = I_2 \otimes \bv{P}(1)$; the payload added to row $(i,j)$ is $\mu \cdot B^i \tilde{e}_j = [\mu B^i]_{q_j}\, \tilde{e}_j$.
    \item Structure: in RNS storage row $(i, j)$ holds the single native polynomial $[\mu B^i]_{q_j}$ in tower $j$; all other towers of the payload are zero. Collecting the nonzero towers across rows reassembles the RNS representations of $\mu, \mu B, \dots, \mu B^{\ell - 1}$:
    % FLAG: earlier notes wrote (\mu, \mu B, \dots, \mu B^{\ell}) --- off by one; the gadget has \ell entries with powers up to B^{\ell-1}.
    \begin{equation*}
        \bv{P}(\mu) \;\cong\;
        \begin{bmatrix}
            [\mu]_{q_0} & \cdots & [\mu B^{\ell - 1}]_{q_0} \\
            \vdots & \ddots & \vdots  \\
            [\mu]_{q_{k-1}} & \cdots & [\mu B^{\ell - 1}]_{q_{k-1}}
        \end{bmatrix}
        = \left([\mu]_{\mathcal{B}_Q}, \dots, [\mu B^{\ell - 1}]_{\mathcal{B}_Q}\right)
    \end{equation*}
    \item Consequently no decomposition is performed at encryption time: row $(i,j)$ requires one native tower product $[\mu]_{q_j} \cdot [B^i]_{q_j}$, computed directly in the NTT domain and added into tower $j$ of component $0$ (rows $0, \dots, k\ell - 1$) resp.\ component $1$ (rows $k\ell, \dots, 2k\ell - 1$) of a fresh encryption of zero.
    \item The factors $[B^i]_{q_j}$ are precomputed once per context ($k\ell$ scalars).
    \item The $k\ell$ row pairs are independent; \autoref{alg:bv:encrypt} parallelizes over them.
    \item \Call{MakePublicRGSW}{} is identical except $Z = 0$: the rows are zero polynomials carrying only the payload tower (and the encryption metadata).
\end{itemize}
