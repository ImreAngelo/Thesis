\subsection{Internal Product}
The internal product is trivially implemented as repeated external products as per the definition~\autoref{def:prod:internal}. Each of the $2k\ell$ rows of the left operand is an independent \gls{rlwe} ciphertext, so the rows are distributed across threads. Nested parallelism is disabled, meaning the stages inside \autoref{alg:external-product} collapse to a single thread when it is invoked from here; a standalone external product keeps its own parallelism.

%%%
%%% Internal Product
%%%
\begin{algorithm}
\caption{Internal Product}
\label{alg:internal-product}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require RGSW ciphertexts $L \in R_Q^{2k\ell}$ and $R \in R_Q^{2k\ell}$
\Ensure RGSW ciphertext of the product
\State Initialize a vector of $2k\ell$ empty RLWE ciphertexts $acc$
\For{$i \gets [0, 2k\ell)$ \textbf{in parallel}}
    \State $acc[i] \gets \Call{EvalExternalProduct}{acc[i],\; R}$
\EndFor
\State \Return $acc$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
