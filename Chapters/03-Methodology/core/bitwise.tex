\subsubsection{Bitwise Operations}
\label{sec:bitwise}
We take the bit-length $b = \log{B}$ as a parameter rather than the base $B$ itself. By restricting the base to a power-of-two $B = 2^b$, we can substitute certain computationally expensive arithmetic operations with fast, hardware-native bitwise operations. They operate independently on each bit of a machine word without the need for carry propagation, additionally making then highly conducive to \gls{simd} vectorization.\footnote{As an added bonus, replacing branching paths with bitwise masks guarantees constant-time execution, aligning with cryptographic best practices to prevent timing side-channels. This is generally not an issue in \gls{fhe} as the entire circuit is always evaluated in full, and these operations are called by the server on encrypted values, but it is still a nice fact and worth being mindful of.} % allowing for accelerated bulk reductions across arrays of polynomial coefficients. 

\begin{table}[H]
    \caption{Common bitwise operations}
    \label{tab:bitwise}
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{lcc}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Operation} & \textbf{Notation} & \textbf{Value}   \\
        \midrule
        \Call{LeftShift}{$x, b$}  & $x \ll b$     & $x\cdot 2^b$ \\
        \Call{RightShift}{$x, b$} & $x \gg b$     & $\lfloor x/2^b \rfloor$ \\
        \Call{And}{$x, y$}        & $x \land y$   & $\sum_i x_{(2)}[i] \cdot y_{(2)}[i] \cdot 2^i$ \\
        % \Call{Or}{x, y}        & $x \lor y$   & $...$ \\
        % \color{red}\Call{Modulo}{$x, b$}     & $x \land [(1 \ll b) - 1]$ & $$ \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Functions built around bitwise operations typically involve masking out certain bits using $\Call{And}{value \text{, mask}}$ where the mask is 1 in bit-positions involved in the calculations, and 0 elsewhere. Below we highlight two efficient patterns used throughout the implementation, exploiting the binary structure of $B=2^b$.

%%%
%%% Center residue
%%%
\begin{algorithm}[ht!]
\caption[Center]{\protect\Call{Center}{$x,b$}}
\label{alg:bit:center}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
    \Require $x, b \in \mathbb{Z}$
    \Ensure $[x]_{2^b} \in [-2^{b-1}, 2^{b-1})$
    \State mask $\gets \Call{LeftShift}{1, b} - 1$
    \State half $\gets \Call{LeftShift}{1, b-1}$
    \State\Return $\Call{And}{x + \text{half},\; \text{mask}} - \text{half}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

%%%
%%% Modulo
%%%
\begin{algorithm}[ht!]
\caption[Modulo]{\protect\Call{Modulo}{$x,b$}}
\label{alg:bit:modulo}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
    \Require $x, b \in \mathbb{Z}$
    \Ensure $x \bmod{2^b}$
    \State mask $\gets \Call{LeftShift}{1, b} - 1$
    \State\Return $\Call{And}{x,\; \text{mask}}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
