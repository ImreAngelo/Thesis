\subsection{sPAR}
\label{sec:results:bench:spar}
Each function in~\autoref{tab:bench:full} except \texttt{server::Decrypt} is invoked once per client every round, so the times below are per call. The \texttt{client} functions would in practice run in parallel, as each user encrypts and partially decrypts independently.

\begin{table}[H]
    \caption{Measured computation time per call of the sPAR functions, 6 threads. The two client functions are timed per client, the two server functions per invocation.}
    \label{tab:bench:full}
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{ccccc}
        \toprule
        $\mathbf{\eta}$ & \textbf{\texttt{Encrypt}} & \textbf{\texttt{Write}} & \textbf{\texttt{Partial dec.}} & \textbf{\texttt{Final dec.}} \\
        \midrule
        $2$ & $336$~ms & $11.14$~s & $44.7$~ms & $10.1$~ms \\
        $4$ & $573$~ms & $23.27$~s & $80.0$~ms & $20.6$~ms \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

A round comprises one encryption and one partial decryption per client, $\eta$ sequential writes, and a single final decryption, which totals $23.0$~s at $\eta = 2$ and $95.7$~s at $\eta = 4$. The corresponding run at $\eta = 8$ did not complete.
