\chapter{Results and Discussion}
\label{chp:results}

%%
%% INTRODUCTION
\vfill
The parameters proposed alongside the original protocol do not carry a round of \gls{spar} to a correct decryption on BGV. Rather than shrink the circuit to fit them, we selected a much larger parameter set that uses the \gls{rns} context in earnest, and report every measurement under it. Under this set the cost of a round is dominated by the server's write step, which is invoked once per participant and itself scales linearly in $\eta$, making the total server work per round quadratic. Noise constrains the protocol as well; the flooding required by multi-party decryption caps reliable operation at $\eta = 8$ participants unless the decomposition is lengthened at a proportional cost in runtime.

\autoref{sec:results:params} gives the parameter set and the reasoning behind it. \autoref{sec:results:noise} measures noise growth, first for the two products in isolation and then across a full protocol round, establishing how many participants the noise budget admits. \autoref{sec:results:benchmarks} reports timings for both libraries and the scaling behaviour of the write step in the number of participants $\eta$. \autoref{sec:results:discussion} discusses what dominates the cost, and \autoref{sec:results:hybrid} proposes a hybrid-gadget variant that lowers it, together with the obstruction that currently stops the variant from extending to the internal product.
