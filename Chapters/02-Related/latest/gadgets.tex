
\section{Gadgets in RNS (keyswitching)}
Digit decomposition requires $a$ to be in its coefficient representation \eqref{form:coefficient}, does not natively work on residues and is therefore not well suited for \gls{rns}. A few \gls{rns}-native gadgets have been proposed over the years, primarily in the context of key switching, such as \cite{cryptoeprint:2016/510, cryptoeprint:2018/117} based on the BV gadget, and \cite{cryptoeprint:2012/099} based on RNS-base extension. % Hybrid is a combination of the two approaches.
