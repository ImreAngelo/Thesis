OpenFHE does not expose \gls{rgsw} or the external and internal products through its public API. Running \gls{spar} on BGV therefore meant building the products before the protocol. The result is three C++ libraries. The \corelib{} library extends OpenFHE~\cite{cryptoeprint:2022/915} with \gls{rgsw} encryption and both products over an \gls{rns} gadget, and is usable independently of \gls{spar}. The \clientlib{} and \serverlib{} libraries implement the protocol on top of it.

\Cref{sec:impl:structure} shows how the three libraries fit together. \Cref{sec:impl:core} describes the \corelib{} library, from the gadget instantiation and the parallel decomposition through to the two products. \Cref{sec:impl:spar} describes our instantiation of \gls{spar} and where it departs from the original specification. \Cref{sec:impl:setup} describes the harness that produces the measurements of \cref{chp:results} --- what is benchmarked, how noise is extracted, and how thread scaling is extrapolated beyond the cores available.
