\begin{itemize}
    \item Gap: TFHE-style RGSW products (external and internal) were not available in BGV-RNS; a C++ library (\corelib) was developed to add this functionality on top of OpenFHE~\cite{cryptoeprint:2022/915}.
    \item Second artifact: an instantiation of the \gls{spar} protocol~\cite{cryptoeprint:2025/860} (\serverlib, \clientlib) built on \corelib.
    \item The two are independent: \corelib{} is usable on its own, and the \gls{spar} implementation depends only on the abstract interface of \autoref{sec:impl:core}, not on a particular instantiation.
    \item Chapter structure: \autoref{sec:impl:core} documents the library architecture and its canonical (BV) instantiation; \autoref{sec:impl:spar} the \gls{spar} instantiation; \autoref{sec:experimental-setup} the experimental methodology. All measurements are deferred to \autoref{chp:results}.
\end{itemize}
